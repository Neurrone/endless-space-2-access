# Screen roadmap

Living inventory of ES2's screens and what making each accessible involves. Class names are
from `decompiled/Assembly-CSharp/`. Status: ☐ pending, ▶ in progress, ✅ done. Complexity:
S (read + activate a list), M (multiple panels/state), L (novel interaction model).

Cross-cutting, every screen at once: **typing a letter searches the focused Tab stop** and moves
focus to the match (no search key; Up/Down step the matches, Escape clears). Screens get it without
declaring anything — a screen only speaks up to opt OUT (`AllowsTypeahead`, `CapturesRawInput`) or to
offer a scope of its own (`Screen.TypeAheadScope`, for items a collapsed tree has not declared). See
`docs/interaction.md`.

Cross-cutting from day one: `ErrorModalWindow` and generic `GuiModalWindow` confirmations
must always speak — a silent error dialog is a soft-lock for a blind player.
`MessageBoxWindow` (all Ok/Cancel/Alternative confirmations, incl. the 15 s video-settings
countdown and binding conflicts) is covered by `MessageBoxScreen`; `ErrorModalWindow` and
`MessageBoxNonBlockingWindow` are still pending.

## Phase 2 — in-game core loop

Rough order: the loop a player touches every turn, before the screens they touch occasionally.

| Screen | Classes | Cx | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Global HUD (empire, pinned quest, notifications, turn) | `EndTurnWindow`, `EmpireBanner`, `NotificationItemsWindow`, `ControlBanner`, `PinnedQuestWindow` | M | ✅ `GlobalHud` — the clusters the game draws over EVERY view level, declared by each of the four pages under them (galaxy, star system, planet overview, research) in the order they are drawn: `hud:empire` (the banners as measured rows) first, the page's own stops, then `hud:quest`, `hud:tutorial`, `hud:notifications` (only while something is pending) and `hud:turn`. They lived on the galaxy screen until the star-system and planet pages showed the defect — drawn on screen, absent from Tab. `hud:quest` is the panel's own click targets, one node each, in drawn order: the PANEL node reads its drawn words (title, status, objective) with the objective's `QuestObjectiveHint` tooltip indicated and buffered and Enter opens the journal on this quest, then "Show location" and "Unpin quest" as sibling nodes — each declared only while the game DRAWS its icon (the marker is hidden outright for a quest with nowhere to point at).
