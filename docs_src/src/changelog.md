# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Galaxy map: every planet now carries its own cards. Opening a world (`Right`) offers a "Tooltips" section with one entry per output figure, one per anomaly found on it, and one per deposit in its ground — so a deposit finally reads its size, the paragraph the game wrote about it, what it gives per population and the reason your empire may not exploit it yet, where before the whole of it was the word "Hyperium". The same entries are there at every zoom: where the map is drawing the icon the card appears over it, and where it is not the card appears in the bottom-left corner with identical words
- Galaxy map: the three buttons the game swaps in while a juggernaut is terraforming, restoring or reducing an anomaly on a planet are entries at last — the only way to cancel that work from the map — and so is the pirate lair on a world whose system holds one, which was the whole way into pirate diplomacy
- Galaxy map: a planet's review buffer now says what the game only paints as colour — that it is being terraformed or restored and by whom, with the turns left; that an anomaly is being reduced; that it hosts a Sanctuary; that it is a unique world; and, on the card, that an outpost here is dying or that somebody's ghost colony stands on it
- System management: a planet card offers the three buttons it draws along its bottom — pick a specialization improvement, reduce an anomaly, terraform — which were reachable only from the Empire screen before. Its anomalies are entries too, each reading the game's page about the anomaly and jumping to the technology that would let it be reduced
- The senate's population census: each slice of the ring opens onto the four things drawn beside it — which party those people lean towards, what that gives the empire, the law it unlocks and what a population boost would do. A law that comes into force by itself now explains that badge
- A population entry opens the Population Overview window on both pages that draw one, the planet page and the star system page — the game's own click, which no entry had
- The game menu's "Show Game Settings" and "Modify Timer Settings" boxes now read their own explanations
- A hot reload of the mod during development can no longer lose speech: the shutdown hands everything back even when one step of it fails
- Galaxy map: the camera now follows your cursor by one rule instead of three. Walking around inside the same system never moves it again, so a zoom you made yourself with `Backslash` survives the whole of reading that system; closing the branch takes the camera back out as before, and opening it again brings it back in. Stepping from a planet back up onto its own star no longer moves anything — the camera only ever comes closer or crosses to somewhere else, never backs out on its own
- Galaxy map: a card the mod draws for you because the game is drawing no icon for it — a system's Antimatter deposit read from the whole galaxy away, say — now appears in the bottom-left corner of the screen instead of the top-left, sitting on the bottom edge whatever its height.
