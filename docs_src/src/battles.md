# Battles

Most of a battle happens in popups. Watching the cinematic is optional — the setup popup carries a Watch box, and with it unticked the battle resolves the moment it starts and you go straight from setup to report. So the popups are the part every player meets, and they carry everything.

## The setup and report popups

Four popups fight a battle: space setup, space report, ground setup, ground report. None of what they show is written text — a fleet's strength is a coloured arc, a ship's health is a bar, the balance of power is two arcs with no number on them — so the mod reads them from the panels behind the drawing and speaks the game's own words for the outcome, each ship's fate, and every button.

They are walked side by side rather than strictly down the page, with `Alt+Up` and `Alt+Down` crossing between the bands:

- the heading — where this is and who is fighting
- **yours** — your leader, your plan, your fleets, and what you earned
- **theirs** — the same for the other side
- the aftermath
- the controls, including the Watch box and the buttons

A setup popup can be timed. Its countdown is readable but never speaks by itself, because a gauge announcing itself over what you are reading would be worse than silence.

## Watching a space battle

The cinematic is a stream rather than a page: the whole battle is computed before it plays, and what you are watching is that report being replayed. The mod narrates it in tiers, loudest first:

- **The pre-roll gate.** When the battle has finished loading, the game freezes and waits for a keypress, and the only thing saying so is a caption. That caption is read the moment it appears, once — press any key to start the battle.
- **Acts**: the introduction names where this is and who it is against, the main act opens with the balance of power, and the outcome is the game's own word for how it went.
- **Phases**: each phase, with the balance of power again, because how the balance moved is what a sighted player is reading off the arcs.
- **Losses**, gathered into bursts: "four of your ships lost" rather than four names arriving over each other.
- **Progress**, in quarters, so a long battle says something occasionally without narrating a percentage.

Keys here are all the game's: Escape stops watching; pause, faster, slower and reset are its own bindings on Pause, Plus, Minus and Asterisk. The controls it draws — skip to the action, the three camera modes, the battle lens — are a stop of their own.

## Watching a ground battle

The invasion animation is narrated the same way: the introduction names the system and both sides with the strategy each picked, then the phases (bombardment, assault) and rounds, then troop losses in bursts ("three of your infantry lost"), then the game's own outcome title and what happens next. The cells on screen carry a health bar and a percentage and no number, so the counts come from the game's own model of who is still alive.

Escape leaves; slower, faster and reset are the game's own keys. The drawn controls are a stop, because the speed multiplier is written on the reset button and nowhere else.

## The advanced report

The advanced battle report walks a heading, the phases, the damage tables and its buttons, and reads the round-by-round detail the summary popup leaves out.

The advanced setup screen — where you review the plans before a battle — walks its heading, the plans, the statistics and its buttons.
