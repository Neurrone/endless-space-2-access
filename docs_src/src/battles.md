# Battles

Most of a battle happens in popups. Watching the cinematic is optional: the setup popup has a Watch checkbox, and with it unticked the battle resolves immediately and the report popup follows.

## Setup and Report Popups

There are four popups: space setup, space report, ground setup, and ground report. The game shows most of their information as graphics — strength arcs, health bars — so the mod reads the underlying data and speaks the game's own words for the outcome, each ship's fate, and every button.

The popups are organized into bands. Use `Alt+Up` and `Alt+Down` to move between them:

- The heading: where the battle is and who is fighting
- Yours: your leader, your battle plan, your fleets, and what you earned
- Theirs: the same for the other side
- The aftermath
- The controls, including the Watch checkbox and the buttons

A setup popup can have a countdown timer. The timer can be read but does not announce itself.

## Watching a Space Battle

If you watch the battle, the mod narrates it as it plays:

- **The start gate.** Once the battle finishes loading, the game waits for a keypress and shows only a caption saying so. The caption is read once when it appears; press any key to start.
- **Acts**: the introduction names the location and the opponent, the main act opens with the balance of power, and the outcome is the game's own verdict.
- **Phases**: each phase is announced with the balance of power, which is how the battle's progress is shown on screen.
- **Losses**, gathered into groups: `four of your ships lost` rather than four separate names.
- **Progress**, announced in quarters.

All keys here are the game's own: `Escape` stops watching; pause, faster, slower and reset are the game's bindings on `Pause`, `+`, `-` and `*`. The drawn controls — skip to the action, the camera modes, the battle lens — are a Tab stop of their own.

## Watching a Ground Battle

The invasion animation is narrated the same way: the introduction names the system, both sides and their strategies; then the phases (bombardment, assault) and rounds; troop losses in groups (`three of your infantry lost`); then the outcome and what happens next.

`Escape` leaves; slower, faster and reset are the game's own keys. The drawn controls are a Tab stop; the current speed multiplier is written on the reset button.

## The Advanced Report

The advanced battle report has a heading, the phases, the damage tables and buttons, with the round-by-round detail the summary popup leaves out.

The advanced setup screen, where you review battle plans before a battle, has a heading, the plans, the statistics and buttons.
