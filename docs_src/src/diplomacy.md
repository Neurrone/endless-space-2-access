# Diplomacy

## Major Factions

The diplomacy screen has the empires you know, the controls beside them, and the metaplot panel. An empire's row carries its full description — who they are, your relationship, the treaties in force, what they think of you — in the [review buffer](buffers.md). The action strip holds what you can do about it.

Every action here takes effect the moment you press it. There is no extra confirmation, so read the strip before pressing.

## Negotiation

The negotiation screen is where treaties are put together. It has six panels:

- **Pressure**: the standing of the two sides
- **My terms**: what you are offering
- **Their terms**: what you are asking for
- **Contract**: the agreement as it stands
- **Approval**: whether they would accept it
- **Actions**: the buttons

Terms are read in the game's own wording. Closing a negotiation without signing still takes effect in this game, so `Escape` is not a free way out of a session you started.

## Minor Factions

A minor faction's page has its identity, your relation with it, what befriending or assimilating it would give you, the actions, and your treasury.

## Pirates

The pirate page has pirate power, your standing with them, the fleet they are preparing next, the actions, and your treasury.
