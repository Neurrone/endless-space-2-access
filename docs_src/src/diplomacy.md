# Diplomacy

## Major factions

The diplomacy screen walks the empires you know, the controls beside them, and the metaplot panel. An empire's row carries its whole dossier — who they are, what you are to each other, the treaties in force, what they think of you — in the review buffer, and the action strip is what you can do about it.

Every action here posts an order the moment you press it. There is no mod-side confirmation, so read the strip before pressing.

## Negotiation

The negotiation screen is where a treaty is put together, and it walks six panels:

- **Pressure**: the standing of the two sides
- **My terms**: what you are offering
- **Their terms**: what you are asking for
- **Contract**: the agreement as it stands
- **Approval**: whether they would accept it
- **Actions**: the buttons

Terms are read as the game words them. Closing a negotiation without signing still posts an order in this game, so Escape is not a free way out of a session you started.

## Minor factions

A minor faction's page walks its identity, your relation with it, what befriending or assimilating it would give you, the actions, and your treasury.

## Pirates

The pirate page walks pirate power, your standing with them, the fleet they are preparing next, the actions, and your treasury.
