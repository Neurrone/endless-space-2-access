# Empire and Economy

## The empire screen

The empire screen is the one place that lists everything you own. Tab walks:

- **Tabs**: the views the screen offers
- **Systems**: your systems as a table, one row each, with the columns the game sorts by. `Up` from the first row reaches that column's heading, where `Enter` sorts
- **Detail**: the planets, constructibles, construction queue and hangar of the system whose band you have opened — the same panels the star system page draws, so queueing, cancelling, buy-outs and carrying a queue line all behave identically
- **Victory**: how each victory condition stands

`Ctrl+Alt+Enter` on a system row — the row's double click — opens that system's own management page, which is the fastest route from a list of forty systems to the one you want to work on. Opening a row's detail band leaves it open until you leave the screen.

## The economy screen

The economy screen walks its tabs and then the panels of whichever tab is live: trade companies, luxury resources, strategic resources, and your recipes.

The **marketplace** tab adds its own panels: the buy tabs and rows, the sell tabs and rows, the band under each, taxes, the transaction log, the marketplace's own announcements, and the market events. A row's quantity is a stepper — announced as "numeric editable", with `Left` and `Right` adjusting it and the new value spoken.

The marketplace itself is technology-gated. Where your empire has no Galactic Commodities Exchange, the game refuses the tab and says so.

## Recipes

The recipe creation modal walks its heading, the luxuries and strategics you can spend, the project, the effects the recipe would have, and its buttons. Confirm is enabled even for an empty recipe, so check the effects panel before pressing it.
